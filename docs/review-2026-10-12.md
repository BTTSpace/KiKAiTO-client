The baseline specifies a cover carousel; it does not settle text animation, background video or automatic video playback. Confirm the exact effect and materials through a demo. The reference website’s full design is not an agreed deliverable.

The owner requires a project-specific screenshot for every feature and a delivery checklist. Design screenshots are marked as mockups. Demo and acceptance must use screenshots of the demonstrated version, with recordings or an interactive demo for motion.
