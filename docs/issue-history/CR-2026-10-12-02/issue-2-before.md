## English explanation

The English website is the revamp scope. The existing Chinese website is preserved. The v2 plan is the baseline; the category discrepancy remains awaiting confirmation in [the category issue](https://github.com/BTTSpace/KiKAiTO-client/issues/4).

The customer supplies bilingual copy, images, videos and covers, English category names and product mappings. The project owner provides content management, English screens, data extensions and continuity with the Chinese site.

Automatic translation, shopping carts, online checkout and third-party service fees are excluded. The website does not send or track quotation emails.

Progress labels remain 待確認 (awaiting confirmation), 待素材 (awaiting materials), 進行中 (in progress), 待驗收 (awaiting acceptance). The owner closes completed items and checks this overview after acceptance.
