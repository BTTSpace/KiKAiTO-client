## English explanation

Use this repository to confirm requirements, provide materials, review progress and respond to demos. Start with All features and progress, then open the relevant feature.

- Without a GitHub account, contact the project owner through your usual channel and include the feature name or issue link. The owner records your reply.
- After registering, sign in and comment on the relevant issue. Describe the screen you saw and the result you expect; attach screenshots when helpful.
- Provide materials on the feature issue or through your usual channel. Share non-public materials privately.
- Open the demo link and confirm that you reviewed it, or list the changes needed.
- The project owner records the version, check results and customer confirmation before closing an issue.

Registration does not automatically make this repository private. Comments and attachments here are publicly readable.

Source: [v2 baseline scope](https://github.com/BTTSpace/KiKAiTO-client/blob/main/docs/scope-v2.md). The customer supplies materials and confirms open decisions; the owner records replies, checks and acceptance.

# 操作與回覆指南

## 變動項目的閱讀方式

先看[變更批次 CR-2026-10-12-01](https://github.com/BTTSpace/KiKAiTO-client/issues/18)。`updated-review` 是內容變更待閱讀，`bilingual` 是已有中英說明，`scope-change` 是新增範圍待確認。以任一語言在對應議題回覆，由負責人記錄對應回覆後移除變更標籤。

預設顯示完整英文，展開下方中文區塊可閱讀完整中文。每項功能依[溝通檢核](https://github.com/BTTSpace/KiKAiTO-client/blob/main/docs/communication-rules.md)辦理，截圖必須符合原網站設計，缺少合格證據維持待補。

## 中文原始範圍與說明

這裡用來確認改版需求、閱讀進度及回覆展示版本。請先看「全部功能與進度總覽」，再點進要討論的功能。

- 尚未註冊 GitHub：透過原本的聯絡方式告知專案負責人，附上功能名稱或 issue 連結，由負責人代記回覆。
- 完成註冊：登入後在對應 issue 底部留言；說明看到的畫面、期待的結果，需要時附上截圖。
- 提供素材：在對應功能留言或透過原聯絡方式交付，由負責人確認收到。公開期間如有不宜公開的資料，透過原聯絡方式提供。
- 看展示版本：依 issue 的展示連結查看，回覆「已看過，沒有問題」或列出需要調整的地方。
- 結案：由專案負責人記錄版本、檢查結果及客戶回覆後關閉 issue。

註冊後也不代表立即轉私有，依實際溝通需求決定。此處公開的留言與附件可被其他人閱讀。
