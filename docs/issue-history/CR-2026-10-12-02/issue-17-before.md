# Global Distribution or Where to Buy

Request record: [FR-2026-10-12-01, item A, document v1](https://github.com/BTTSpace/KiKAiTO-client/blob/main/docs/customer-feedback/FR-2026-10-12-01/README.md). Change batch: [CR-2026-10-12-01](https://github.com/BTTSpace/KiKAiTO-client/issues/18).

## Customer request and proposed experience

The customer suggests a visible English-homepage entry to promote major distributors worldwide and attract prospective distribution partners. This is added scope awaiting agreement, not an approved implementation.

Suggested flow: open the English-homepage entry, find distributors by country or region, then open an approved distributor website or contact option. A simple directory is proposed first. An interactive map, distributor account system, automatic location detection and checkout are not agreed deliverables.

## Decisions and materials needed

- [ ] Confirm the name Global Distribution or Where to Buy and the navigation/homepage location.
- [ ] Confirm the purpose: network exposure, buying locations, or both.
- [ ] Supply the approved public list: country/region, company, website and contact details.
- [ ] Confirm grouping, order and what visitors should do for unserved regions.
- [ ] Confirm whether prospective partners use the existing contact channel.
- [ ] Confirm whether administrators maintain the directory; this is not yet part of the existing homepage-management promise.
- [ ] The owner records scope, schedule and any cost impact before implementation is scheduled.

## Screen evidence pending

The previous generic mockups were not verified against the existing website design and have been removed. The owner must supply matching project-specific design screenshots before planning is marked ready for delivery. Demo and acceptance require actual desktop/mobile screenshots, key states and the demonstrated version/date. See [communication checks](https://github.com/BTTSpace/KiKAiTO-client/blob/main/docs/communication-rules.md).

## Acceptance after scope agreement

- [ ] Approved distributor details and usable links appear in the agreed locations on desktop and mobile.
- [ ] Actual screenshots match the demonstrated version.
- [ ] The owner records check results, customer confirmation and the completion decision.
